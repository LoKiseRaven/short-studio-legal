Short Studio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Scope

Short Studio is a personal desktop tool that runs only on its author's own computer. It is used to publish videos to the author's own TikTok account. It has no server, no website backend and no other users.

2. Data accessed from TikTok

With the author's authorization through TikTok Login Kit, Short Studio accesses:

  • user.info.basic: open ID, display name and avatar, to show which account is connected;
  • video.upload and video.publish: to send the author's own videos to their TikTok account.

The access token issued by TikTok is stored only on the author's computer.

3. What is not done

No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or sent to any server other than TikTok's own API. No analytics or tracking is used.

4. Retention and deletion

Data stays on the author's computer and can be deleted at any time by deleting the local files. Access can also be revoked at any time from the TikTok app settings (Security > Manage app permissions).
